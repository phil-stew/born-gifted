Privacy Policy

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases.

Data on your device

Your save files, settings, and difficulty choice are stored only on your device, using your browser's local storage. Names you type in the game stay in those saves. None of this is sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it permanently.

Hosting

The game's files are delivered by our hosting provider, Vercel, which may process standard technical information such as IP addresses to serve those files and keep the service secure. We do not use this information to identify you.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
